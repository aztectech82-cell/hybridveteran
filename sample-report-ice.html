Why This Verdict — Mileage Verification

A vehicle-history/title-brand lookup (Carfax, AutoCheck, or an NICB VINCheck for a "TMU" — True Mileage Unknown — title brand) is outside the scope of this on-site mechanical inspection, and for a vehicle this old, those databases are frequently sparse or incomplete anyway. The buyer can pull that report directly at carfax.com or nicb.org/vincheck using the VIN at his convenience; it's a five-minute, low-cost check worth doing before finalizing the purchase, but it is not part of what this inspection covers.

What the Physical Inspection Found — and Why It Supports the Claim

  • The tires are original to the truck (18 years old) and are aged/weathered rather than worn down from use — a genuinely high-mileage truck would have gone through at least one full set of tires by now.
  • Body panels and paint show no evidence of accident repair; light chips/scratches read as good patina for a 2007, not heavy-use wear.
  • Odometer cluster shows no signs of tampering (mismatched fonts, tool marks, gaps) — "looks very original."
  • Physical wear correlation (pedals, seat bolster, carpet, sill plates) reads consistent with the claimed low mileage.
  • Engine bay condition is clean with minimal corrosion, and the differential appears to have been serviced recently.
  • No stored or pending DTCs, all readiness monitors ready, no ABS faults.

Age-Related, Not Mileage-Related (18 Years Old Regardless of Miles Driven)

  • Battery is date-coded 2022 (not original) and reads weak at 11.7V resting with terminal corrosion; alternator charges fine at 13.4V.
  • Rust coloration present is surface/age-related patina, not active structural corrosion.
  • Rear bushings need replacement — rubber component wear from age, not mileage.

Recommended Follow-Up (Regardless of Mileage Verdict)

  • Brake fluid flush — fluid tested discolored and has an odor consistent with moisture contamination.
  • Transmission drain & refill as a precaution — no record of one ever being performed.
  • Battery replacement — date-coded 2022, testing weak.
  • Rear suspension bushings — age-related wear, budget for replacement.
  • Tire replacement — original tires, 18 years old, aged out regardless of low mileage.

A written verdict, backed by what was physically found — not just a yes or no.
