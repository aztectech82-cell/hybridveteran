What we watch for on Temecula Valley cars

A lot of cars here have spent years on the I-15 and I-215 commute. Highway miles are not automatically bad miles, but they show up in tires, brakes, suspension, and wheel bearings, so the road test includes highway speed where it is safe to do so. On an EV we read the range estimate after the car has warmed up instead of trusting a cold number.

Valley summers also work cooling systems and A/C hard. We check both, and on a hybrid we look at the battery cooling system where it can be reached.

Who inspects the car

Every Hybrid Veteran inspection follows the same written checklist, in the order the work is actually done: walk-around, a cold under-hood check, a scan before the engine starts, start-up and warm-up, a road test, the interior, and then a powertrain-specific check for gas, hybrid, or EV. Before a report goes out, it is reviewed by Marcos Alvarez Guzman, an Army veteran with ASE certifications A2, A4, A5, A6, A7, and A8, under BAR Automotive Repair Dealer registration 302636.

What you get

  • A written condition report, not a verbal opinion.
  • Every item marked Pass, Attention, Fail, or Not Tested, with the reason.
  • Photos tied to each material finding.
  • Scan results, including stored and permanent fault codes and monitor readiness.
  • Hybrid or EV battery data and warning status when the exact vehicle and scan tool support it.
  • A plain statement of what could not be checked and why.

See a real one first: hybrid battery fault sample report, mileage verification sample report, and the field inspection sheet.

The inspection is non-destructive. We do not open a high-voltage battery pack, unplug orange cables, or promise remaining battery life. If something needs a lift, a longer drive, or a dealer-level tool, the report says so.

Common Questions

Do you inspect at Murrieta and Temecula dealerships?

Yes, with the dealer's permission. Private-party cars are inspected at the seller's location with the seller's permission.

Is high highway mileage a deal breaker?

Not by itself. The report shows what the miles actually did to tires, brakes, suspension, and fluids, so you can judge the car on its condition.

Can you check a hybrid or EV battery?

When the exact vehicle and scan tool support it, we record battery data, stored codes, and warning status. We do not open the pack or guarantee remaining battery life.

Is this a smog check?

No. The report includes scan and monitor readiness data, but it is not a smog inspection.
