What we watch for on Inland Empire cars

Inland heat is hard on 12-volt batteries, cooling systems, and air conditioning, and it is one of the main things that ages a hybrid battery. On a hybrid we look at the battery cooling intake and fan where they can be reached, check A/C output on the road test, and record battery block voltages and stored hybrid codes when the vehicle supports it.

Shopping more than one car? One visit can cover each vehicle against the same checklist, which makes it easier to compare a car in Riverside against one in Corona on equal terms.

Who inspects the car

Every Hybrid Veteran inspection follows the same written checklist, in the order the work is actually done: walk-around, a cold under-hood check, a scan before the engine starts, start-up and warm-up, a road test, the interior, and then a powertrain-specific check for gas, hybrid, or EV. Before a report goes out, it is reviewed by Marcos Alvarez Guzman, an Army veteran with ASE certifications A2, A4, A5, A6, A7, and A8, under BAR Automotive Repair Dealer registration 302636.

What you get

  • A written condition report, not a verbal opinion.
  • Every item marked Pass, Attention, Fail, or Not Tested, with the reason.
  • Photos tied to each material finding.
  • Scan results, including stored and permanent fault codes and monitor readiness.
  • Hybrid or EV battery data and warning status when the exact vehicle and scan tool support it.

See a real one first: hybrid battery fault sample report, mileage verification sample report, and the field inspection sheet.

The inspection is non-destructive. We do not open a high-voltage battery pack, unplug orange cables, or promise remaining battery life. If something needs a lift, a longer drive, or a dealer-level tool, the report says so.

Common Questions

Do you inspect cars at Riverside and Corona dealerships?

Yes, with the dealer's permission. Many dealers allow an independent inspection on the lot. If a dealer refuses one, that tells you something about the car.

Can you check a used Prius or other hybrid battery?

Where the vehicle and scan tool support it, we record battery block voltages, stored hybrid codes, and warning status. We do not open the battery pack or guarantee remaining battery life.

Is this a smog check?

No. The report includes scan and monitor readiness data, but it is not a smog inspection or a promise that the car will pass one.

How soon can you get out to the car?

Riverside is home base, so it usually has the shortest wait. Evening and weekend times are limited, so call as soon as you have a car picked out.
