What the Standard Inspection Covers

The report separates observed findings from items that were inaccessible, unsupported, unsafe, or not authorized.

Identity & Operating Check

  • VIN and odometer documentation
  • Cold start when arranged in advance
  • Warning indicators and observable operation
  • Road test when authorized and safe

Engine & Powertrain

  • Visible fluid levels and condition
  • Observable leaks and abnormal sounds
  • Accessible cooling components, belts, and hoses
  • Operation under available test conditions

Exterior & Underbody

  • Body, glass, lamps, and visible damage
  • Observable signs of prior repair or corrosion
  • Accessible frame and underbody areas
  • Visible boots, lines, and driveline components

Brakes & Tires

  • Visible brake components where accessible
  • Brake feel during an authorized road test
  • Tire tread, date codes, and wear patterns
  • Steering and suspension observations

Electrical, HVAC & Interior

  • Accessible controls and displays
  • HVAC operation under current conditions
  • Interior condition and safety equipment
  • 12-volt system data when supported

Diagnostic Scan

  • OBD-II codes and readiness information when available
  • Manufacturer-module access when supported
  • Public recall lookup using the supplied VIN
  • Findings and scan limitations recorded in writing

Ask About Your Vehicle

Common Questions

  • Do you inspect gas and diesel trucks, not just hybrids and EVs?

    Yes. The inspection and report are built around your specific concerns on any vehicle, gas, diesel, hybrid, or electric, from compact cars to light-duty pickups, SUVs, and vans. For commercial trucks over 14,000 lb, our sister business VetFleet handles heavy-duty inspections and Clean Truck Check.

  • What does a pre-purchase inspection check?

    A pre-purchase inspection checks a vehicle's observable condition, systems, and available diagnostic data before you buy — body, undercarriage, suspension, and a full OBD-II scan, documented with photos and a written report. Hybrid and EV vehicles get vehicle-reported battery and charging data added within the same visit when supported.

  • How long does a mobile pre-purchase inspection take?

    Most single-vehicle inspections are completed at the seller's location in one visit; exact timing depends on vehicle access, powertrain, and scope confirmed before the appointment. We plan the appointment window with you in advance, not on arrival.

  • What's checked in a hybrid or EV battery inspection?

    We check vehicle-reported hybrid, EV, charging, and battery-system warning indicators, available scan data and parameters, accessible 12-volt battery condition, and visible cables, connectors, and cooling components — within the standard visit, not a separate appointment. This is not automatically a laboratory battery-capacity test; any unavailable test is stated plainly in the report.

  • Can I get more than one car inspected in one visit?

    Yes — multi-vehicle work is confirmed case by case based on vehicle type, access, location, and timing, using one consistent reporting approach across the vehicles compared in the same trip.
