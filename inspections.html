What Every Inspection Covers

Bumper to bumper, plus a road test and a full diagnostic scan. Nothing skipped, nothing eyeballed.

Road Test

  • Cold start, idle, acceleration
  • Shift quality, braking, steering
  • Suspension, alignment feel
  • Noise and vibration

Engine & Powertrain

  • Fluids, condition, leaks
  • Cooling system, belts, hoses
  • Battery load test, charging output
  • Exhaust, mounts

Under Vehicle

  • Frame, unibody, corrosion
  • Evidence of prior collision repair
  • Driveline, CV boots, differential
  • Fuel lines, steering linkage

Brakes & Tires

  • Pad/shoe thickness, measured in mm
  • Rotors, lines, parking brake
  • Tread depth per corner, in 32nds
  • DOT date codes, wear pattern

Electrical, HVAC, Interior

  • Every function tested
  • A/C vent temperature recorded
  • Suspension, steering, sway bar links
  • Exterior condition

Diagnostic Scan

  • Stored, pending, permanent codes
  • Readiness monitors
  • Open recall check
  • Written severity coding: Pass / Monitor / Repair / Safety
