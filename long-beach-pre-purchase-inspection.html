What matters on a Long Beach car

Cars that live near the coast deal with salt air, which can speed up rust on the underbody, brake lines, and electrical connectors. We photograph the underbody and, on a hybrid or EV, the battery enclosure wherever it can be seen without taking anything apart.

Long Beach also has a busy private-party market, and many of those cars are parked on the street. Before booking we confirm a safe, legal place to work and a route for the road test. If the car cannot be inspected safely where it sits, you hear that before the appointment, not after.

Who inspects the car

Every Hybrid Veteran inspection follows the same written checklist, in the order the work is actually done: walk-around, a cold under-hood check, a scan before the engine starts, start-up and warm-up, a road test, the interior, and then a powertrain-specific check for gas, hybrid, or EV. Before a report goes out, it is reviewed by Marcos Alvarez Guzman, an Army veteran with ASE certifications A2, A4, A5, A6, A7, and A8, under BAR Automotive Repair Dealer registration 302636.

What you get

  • A written condition report, not a verbal opinion.
  • Every item marked Pass, Attention, Fail, or Not Tested, with the reason.
  • Photos tied to each material finding.
  • Scan results, including stored and permanent fault codes and monitor readiness.
  • Hybrid or EV battery data and warning status when the exact vehicle and scan tool support it.
  • A plain statement of what could not be checked and why.

See a real one first: hybrid battery fault sample report, mileage verification sample report, and the field inspection sheet.

The inspection is non-destructive. We do not open a high-voltage battery pack, unplug orange cables, or promise remaining battery life. If something needs a lift, a longer drive, or a dealer-level tool, the report says so.

Common Questions

Can you inspect a car that is parked on the street?

Often, if it can be done safely and legally. Street parking can limit the underbody view and the road test, and the report notes anything that could not be checked.

Do you come to dealers in Long Beach, Signal Hill, and Downey?

Yes, with the dealer's permission. An independent inspection is worth asking for even when the dealer has already certified the car.

Can you check a used EV battery?

When the exact vehicle and scan tool support it, we record battery data and warning status. The dashboard range estimate alone is not a battery health reading.

Do you go to Torrance or Orange County?
